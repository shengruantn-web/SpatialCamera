# Run log: S5 delivered size, PLY vs SPZ (2026-11-22)

**Shipment**: S5 "Fast to view" ([delivery plan](../M0-M1-delivery.md)) · **Related**: ADR-0006, ADR-0007, spec §3 and §7.3

## Input

The Brush (Apple Silicon) result from the S4 proof run `.local/runs/20261122-212521`: 64 photos of COLMAP's public *South Building* set (1600 px), 64/64 registered, 39,901 sparse points. Align took 347 s and train 1028 s (7000 steps). The export is `export/result.ply`: binary little-endian, 233,586 splats, SH degree 2, 38 float properties (normals included).

## Size

| Delivered file | Bytes | vs PLY | Download at 50 Mbit/s | at 10 Mbit/s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `result.ply` (pre-S5 viewer download, now the archive) | 35,506,098 | 1× | 5.7 s | 28.4 s |
| PLY with gzip -6 (the "transfer encoding" alternative in ADR-0007) | 33,550,782 | 1.06× | 5.4 s | 26.8 s |
| **`result.spz`** (what the viewer now loads) | **4,979,356** | **7.13×** | **0.8 s** | **4.0 s** |

The SPZ is v3 with 12 fractional bits. Its payload is 10,277,800 bytes before gzip. `gpu-worker/spz.py` converted it in 2.4 s on the M-series host, which is under 0.2% of this job's 1,375 s of align plus train. ADR-0007's target of under 15 MB delivered is met with room to spare. Gzip barely compresses float PLY data, which confirms the ADR's reason for rejecting that alternative.

## Fidelity

Both files were decoded with Spark's own WASM decoder, the one the viewer runs, and compared splat by splat (all 233,586):

| Attribute | Median error | p99 | Max | Note |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Centre (scene units; coordinates up to ±18.5) | 9.7e-5 | 1.2e-4 | 1.2e-4 | 24-bit fixed point, step 2^-12 |
| Colour (rgb 0–1) | 0.0029 | 0.0039 | 0.50 | The max is 1,494 of 700,758 channels whose PLY value is above 1.44 (over-white). SPZ's DC range clamps them, and they still render clipped white |
| Opacity | 0.0010 | 0.0020 | 0.0024 | 8-bit |
| Log scale | 0.025 | 2.3 | 5.5 | Every error over 0.1 (17,084 of 700,758 axes) is an axis with log scale < -10, i.e. thinner than 4.5e-5 units. SPZ's 8-bit log scale starts at -10, and the reference encoder clamps the same way |
| Rotation (degrees) | 0.044 | 0.51 | 3.6 | v3 smallest-three, 9-bit |

SH degree 1 keeps 5 bits and degree 2 keeps 4, as in the reference encoder. `tests/spz-roundtrip.test.mjs` checks these bounds on every CI run, using Spark's decoder for v2 and v3.

## Viewer (browser smoke, headless Chromium)

This was a manual check, not a CI test (deferred, see the S5 report). The dev server ran with a mocked capture API, and the scene route streamed this SPZ in 256 KB chunks with a `Content-Length`:

- The viewer requested `/api/captures/cap1/scene?v=<ready_at>`. It showed real byte progress from "Downloading · 0.3 MB of 4.7 MB" through "4.5 MB of 4.7 MB", then "Preparing your scene…", and then rendered 234k splats.
- The result panel offers "Download PLY · 33.9 MB" as the full-precision archive.
- Switching scenes PLY, SPZ, PLY through the file picker kept one canvas and one WebGL context, with no page errors. The only other context came from the viewer remounting on a tab change, and that context is now released with `forceContextLoss()`.
- With one `ready` capture and nothing active, `/api/captures` was polled twice in the whole run (load and the tab open). Polling stops at terminal states.
